How to Play

Before a round, review your crews and their effective stats, then every planned problem. Preparation is untimed. Choose Start Round when ready; problems activate gradually on the schedule.

During play, open Crew stats (R) or Problem schedule (O) to consult the full reference. Time keeps running while you read. Choose Pause to stop time. Retry returns to the untimed briefing.

Storms are damaging the city. Click an incident, pick the right crew and send it along the roads to fix it before things get worse. Unrepaired incidents turn urgent and wear down the city's condition. Repair 10 incidents before the storm ends.
