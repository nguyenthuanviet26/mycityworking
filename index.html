Keep the city running through the rainy season

How to Play

Storms are damaging the city. Click an incident, pick the right crew and send it along the roads to fix it before things get worse. Unrepaired incidents turn urgent and wear down the city's condition. Repair 10 incidents before the storm ends.

Your crews

Each crew is Best at one kind of damage. A crew that can only help takes twice as long and gets no Right-crew bonus.
